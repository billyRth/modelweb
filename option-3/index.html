One job, four moves.

Follow a single booking through Roster, from a phone in a casting lobby to money in the model's account.

1

Digitals in

A model shoots polaroids on her phone. Her card and board tile refresh before she leaves the lobby.

2

Package out

The booker pulls six faces into a casting package and sends the client one link.

3

Option to confirm

The client approves two. Options land on the calendar in order, and the rest release on their own.

Invoice to payout

The job wraps. Roster drafts the invoice, takes commission, and queues the talent payout.
