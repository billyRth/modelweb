How it works

Brief in. Answers out.

Three steps replace the email chains, LINE threads and one-off forms.

STEP 01

Brief

The agency enters the client's requirements once: age, ethnicity, hair, gender, experience, measurements, shoot and audition dates, and the reply deadline.

STEP 02

Match & send

Roster finds every model who fits and is free on those dates, then sends one request to all of them. Busy models are skipped, not bothered.

STEP 03

Answers per date

Models tap 1st keep, 2nd keep or NG for each date beside their own calendar. The agency sees every answer on one dashboard, and talks details in chat.
