How it works

From board to payout in four moves.

Most agencies are fully live within two weeks. We migrate your board, your clients and your open options for you.

STEP 01

Import your board

Bring talent, measurements, digitals and divisions over from spreadsheets or your old system. Our concierge team handles the heavy lifting.

STEP 02

Build a package

Drag faces into a branded casting package, add notes and rates, and send a single elegant link instead of twelve attachments.

STEP 03

Lock the option

Clients approve in one tap. Options move from second to first to confirmed, and the talent's calendar updates everywhere at once.

STEP 04

Invoice and pay out

Roster writes the invoice from the booking, chases it politely, and splits commission and payout the moment funds land.
